# Activity 2: Business Concept and Graphic Design

## Overview
This activity focused on conceptualizing a business idea and translating it into a visual design using graphic design tools.

## Key Concepts and Skills Gained
I learned how to conceptualize a business idea and translate it into a visual design through graphic design tools. This activity allowed me to explore the creative process of developing a business concept and presenting it in a way that would appeal to potential customers.

## Challenges Encountered and Solutions
One of the challenges I encountered was deciding which business idea to focus on. I had difficulty choosing a concept that I could develop and turn into a design. I addressed this by exploring different possibilities and considering which idea would be more practical and interesting to present.

## Future Applications
I could apply what I learned if I decide to pursue a business in the future. It also helped me realize that graphic design can be useful not only for business purposes but also for developing and presenting creative ideas in different fields.
