# Activity 7: Resume Creation and Formatting

## Overview
This activity focused on creating and formatting a professional resume with the assistance of AI tools.

## Key Concepts and Skills Gained
I learned how to properly format and organize a resume to make it look professional and present relevant information effectively. This activity helped me understand the importance of presenting my educational background, skills, and experiences in a clear and organized manner.

## Challenges Encountered and Solutions
One challenge I encountered was dealing with inaccuracies in some of the information generated by AI. There were certain details that did not accurately reflect my actual background and experiences. To address this, I reviewed the generated content carefully, provided additional instructions, and prompted the AI again to correct the errors.

## Future Applications
I can apply what I learned when creating an actual resume for future job applications, particularly when applying for a nursing position after graduation. This activity helped me become more aware of how to present my qualifications and experiences professionally, which will be important as I prepare to enter the workforce.
