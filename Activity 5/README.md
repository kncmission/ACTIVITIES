# Activity 5: Exploring AI Large Language Models

## Overview
This activity explored different AI large language models (LLMs), their capabilities, and how they can be utilized to assist with various tasks.

## Key Concepts and Skills Gained
I realized that different AI large language models (LLMs) have their own strengths and weaknesses. Through this activity, I learned that each AI tool may produce different results depending on the task and the way instructions are given. I also gained a better understanding of how important proper prompting is in getting accurate and useful responses.

## Challenges Encountered and Solutions
One challenge I encountered was getting the exact output I wanted from the AI. Sometimes, it took several attempts to construct an effective prompt and achieve the desired results. I addressed this by modifying my instructions, adding more specific details, and refining my prompts until the output better matched my expectations.

## Future Applications
I can use AI tools to assist and guide me in my everyday tasks, particularly when studying, researching, and organizing information. However, I also learned the importance of reviewing and verifying AI-generated information rather than relying on it completely.
